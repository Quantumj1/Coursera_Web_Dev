About Us

Our Story

David Chu's China Bistro is a family-owned restaurant specializing in authentic Chinese cuisine. For over a decade, we have been serving the Baltimore area with recipes passed down through generations, using only the freshest ingredients.

From our famous hand-folded dumplings to our slow-braised dishes, every plate is prepared with care and a passion for flavor. Our commitment to quality has made us a beloved neighborhood destination and earned us the trust of our guests — as well as our Kosher certification.

Our Mission

We believe that great food brings people together. Our mission is to provide a warm, welcoming atmosphere where families and friends can enjoy delicious, authentic Chinese dishes made from the highest quality ingredients — all at prices that make every visit worthwhile.
